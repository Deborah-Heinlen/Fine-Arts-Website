Redbuds
in Bloom

"Some things announce themselves
without apology."

Delicate redbud blossoms cluster along a single branch in early spring, their purple-pink flowers announcing the season with quiet insistence. Rendered with a watercolor finish from my original photograph, this print celebrates the ephemeral beauty of flowering trees, soft, familiar, and alive with the gentle color of renewal.
