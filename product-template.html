No. 01  —  Botanical Studies

Water Lily in Bloom

A water lily rests serenely on calm pond waters, its purple petals reflected in the glassy surface alongside lily pads and natural debris. Captured through my lens and rendered with a watercolor finish, this print celebrates the quiet beauty of aquatic gardens. The reflections and floating elements create a peaceful composition of stillness.
