Redbuds
in Bloom

"Some things announce themselves
without apology."

Delicate redbud blossoms cluster along a single branch in early spring, their purple-pink flowers announcing the season with quiet insistence. Rendered with a watercolor finish from my original photograph, this print celebrates the ephemeral beauty of flowering trees -- soft, familiar, and alive with the gentle color of renewal.

About Your Print

Medium 01

Metal Print

Each print is infused directly into specially coated aluminum, bringing your image to life with vibrant color, luminescent depth, and exceptional detail. The ultra-hard surface is scratch-resistant, waterproof, and arrives ready to hang with a float mount hanger. Production time is 2-3 business days.

Medium 02

Canvas Print

Each print is produced directly onto premium fine art canvas material, then wrapped around a 1.5 inch stretcher frame for a contemporary, gallery-style display. Printed with a lustre finish, each canvas arrives ready to hang with wire hardware attached. Production time is 2-3 business days.

Medium 03

Framed Print

Each print is produced on lustre fine art paper and hand-inspected before being mounted in a genuine solid wood frame. Frames are available in flat black, flat white, flat mocha, and flat natural basswood, each with a naturally rich finish. Choose your mat color from polar white, white, steel blue, chocolate brown, or smoke gray to complement your space. Each framed print arrives ready to hang with wire hardware attached. Production time is 5-7 business days.
