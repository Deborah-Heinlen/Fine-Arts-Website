Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all Deborah Heinlen Photography LLC ("DHP") websites, applications, and services, including our fine art print shop. Please review this policy to understand your rights, our standards, and the regulations that govern your privacy.

Our Commitment to Data Privacy

At DHP, the privacy of your data is a top priority. We take extreme care with any data that directly identifies you, such as your name or address, or any data that can reasonably be used to identify you.

This notice does not apply to third parties or affiliates. For information on how third parties handle your data, please review their individual privacy policies.

What Personal Data We Collect

DHP collects personal information when you interact with our websites, place an order for photography services or fine art prints, or sign up for our marketing lists. This may include:

Information you provide to us

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mailing and shipping address
  • Phone number
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment partners)

Information collected automatically

  • Purchase and cart history
  • IP address and access time
  • Device and browser usage data
  • Cookie and pixel data
  • Location data

Information from third parties

  • Fraud detection and credit data
  • Demographic and activity data from partners

How We Use Your Data

DHP uses your personal information to provide and improve our services, process your transactions, fulfill your orders, communicate with you, and comply with applicable legal requirements. For fine art print orders, your information is shared with our print fulfillment partners to produce and ship your custom order.

How We Share Your Data

DHP may share your information with the following parties as necessary to fulfill your orders and provide our services:

  • E-commerce platforms (Squarespace) for website hosting and payment processing
  • Print fulfillment partners (Printique) for art print production and shipping
  • Payment service providers for secure transaction processing
  • Delivery companies for shipping and tracking
  • Marketing services, limited to your name and postal address only

DHP will never sell your personal information. In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the relevant organization.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to know, access, correct, transfer, restrict the processing of, and delete your personal information at any time. You may also request to be removed from our marketing lists and cease all email communications at any time. You will not be subject to any discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

To make a request, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section below. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies to provide a secure user experience, keep your shopping cart persistent, and help us understand how our site is being used. You may opt out of cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Children's Privacy

DHP does not knowingly collect information from any person under the age of 18. Our services are not directed to children.

How We Protect Your Data

DHP employs reasonable electronic, physical, and operational measures to ensure your personal information is secure and protected from unauthorized use, disclosure, modification, or loss.

Privacy Regulations

DHP works to maintain compliance with applicable data privacy regulations, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and other applicable international privacy laws.

Updates to This Policy

DHP reserves the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Updates will be reflected on this page. If changes are substantial, we may notify you by email. Please review this policy periodically.
