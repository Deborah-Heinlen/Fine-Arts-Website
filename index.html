Every Print Begins as a Photograph.

Every photograph becomes something more. I photograph the natural world -- botanicals, wildlife, the quiet rhythms of farm and field -- then transform each image into a watercolor fine art print. The result is something between memory and observation: familiar yet painterly, grounded in a real moment yet alive with color and texture.
